This is a campaign for RENEW by PVA which is a scripture meditation app.

The idea is to shoot three simple 30-second visuals, one in the Lifestyle Studio (with the couch) and two in Studio B (the white cove space studio).

Each visual shows the actors go from a state of chaos to a state of order after meditating on a verse from scripture.

VISUAL 1 - ANDREA ONIFADE

Visual 1 shows an Italian man in a vest doing an aggressive workout, moving from a state of hurry to a state of calm.

He does a few push ups, sit ups, squats, room pacing, sitting and walking.

This is mostly tracking shots and quick cuts to show intensity.

VISUAL 2 - SOVEREIGN "SOVE" SECONDUS

Visual 2 shows a woman dealing with anxiety but moving to a place of peace as she meditates on the Word.

This has more close up shots showing shaking hands, rapid-blinking eyes, tense neck muscles.

This is mostly close-up shots and wide-angle shots.

VISUAL 3 - DEAN "DEANO" DOBSON

Visual 3 shows a man dealing with hopelessness but moving to a place of joy as he meditates on the Word.

This has still close up shots showing the man's face moving from despair to joy. A tracking shot showing struggle against the room to face a wall.

This is mostly close-up shots and a tracking shot.
